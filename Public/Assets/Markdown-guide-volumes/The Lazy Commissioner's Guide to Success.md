The pinnacle of the "Lazy" strategy is the transition to Regional Governor. This role shifts focus from managing local assets to overseeing a regional alliance. Governance is achieved by controlling two or more territories individually or through proposed Regional Alliances.
The Triple Friction Points of Governance
As an executive, you must navigate three distinct 1% levies that maintain the ecosystem's liquidity:
The 1% Exchange Fee: Applied to all Entity Market trades.
The 1% Luxury Tax: Applied to all Master Tier/Governor gear purchases.
The 1% Governor Surcharge: A penalty of 1% of the total club treasury, paid whenever a Governor enacts or adjusts localized tax policies.
The Governor’s Passive Revenue Streams

The Governor of the Arena Center
