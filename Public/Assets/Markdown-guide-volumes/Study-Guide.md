Section 2: Answer Key
Manager Responsibilities: The Manager is responsible for the administrative and financial oversight of a club's commerce. Their specific duties include adjusting shop commission rates for localized storefronts and overseeing inventory logs to monitor the flow of items within the organization.
Combat Rules: The Same Rule is triggered when a card’s directional values match the adjacent values of cards already on the board on two or more sides, capturing them regardless of power. The Plus Rule occurs when the sum of a card’s directional value and an adjacent card's value is equal on two or more sides, resulting in a capture.
Quadratic Slippage: This mechanism applies accelerating (exponential) costs to large trade orders in the Entity Market, making massive market-altering trades significantly more expensive than smaller, tactical ones. This prevents "whales" from using raw capital to dominate valuations, ensuring share prices are instead driven by match victories and reputation.
Regional Governor Requirements: A player must first establish a Club by paying a fixed fee to claim territory. To be upgraded to a Region and earn the title of Regional Governor, the owner must control two or more territories, either through individual expansion or by forming a regional alliance with other owners.
Mutation Scars: A Mutation Scar is an irreversible result of a failed genetic modification procedure in the Mutation Foundry. It causes a permanent reduction of 50 Artifact power points, is archived immutably on the blockchain for public visibility, and lowers the card's Forensic Grading.
Lease Board Revenue: Total rental revenue is divided through a multi-way split: 50% is paid to the Lender as passive income, 20% is routed to the Global Faucet as a tax, 20% goes to the Lender's Club Treasury, and the remaining 10% is distributed among the club members.
Symmetrical Liability Shift: This process maintains economic parity between platforms; when a console player wins against a browser player, the loser's $VBV is routed to the Global Faucet while the winner receives an equivalent 1:1 increment in Arena Vouchers. The physical $VBV remains in the Arena Vault while the console player receives non-crypto spending power for DLC and hardware.
Kidnap Gambit and Safety: This high-stakes mechanic allows a perpetrator to target a victim's favorite or rarest card for ransom, causing immediate reputation shifts for both parties. Victims can either pay the ransom (subject to a 20% Laundering Tax) or wait for the 48-hour "Insurance" auto-return protocol to recover the asset for free.
Mutation Protocols and Costs: The three protocols are Vector Realignment (500 $VBV) for re-allocating directional power, Mood Recalibration (250 $VBV + 1 Mood Catalyst) for changing a card's native element, and Loyalty Synthesis (1,000 $VBV) for instantly reaching 100% loyalty.
Infrastructure Siphon: The 10% Infrastructure Siphon extracts a portion of all DLC revenue generated via console-native payment systems. These funds are allocated to a maintenance ledger used specifically to fund hardware costs, software licenses, and server power required to sustain the console ecosystem.

Section 4: Comprehensive Glossary
Term
Definition
$VBV
The primary currency of the Arena, pegged 1:1 with $VOI and used for all major industrial and market transactions.
Arena Vouchers
A 1:1 virtual representation of $VBV used on consoles to maintain non-crypto compliance while allowing players to buy DLC and hardware.
Bounty Board
A tracking system for "Outlaws" (Wanted Level 10+) that allows "Clean Hunters" to collect $VBV rewards for defeating them.
Clerk
A role-gated operational job focused on accelerating store turnover speeds to optimize a club's revenue flow.
Cloak Disruptor
A tactical item used by Bounty Hunters to temporarily reveal the signal of an Outlaw attempting to hide via Ghost Protocol.
Cunning
A player attribute that scales heist success rates and mitigates combat power penalties associated with high Wanted Levels.
Cyber-Audit
An intelligence item used to probe a target club to reveal its treasury average and current economic crash status.
Cyber-Lock
A high-tier hardware trap that encrypts treasury data and prevents all Cyber-Audits for 24 hours.
Entity Market
A specialized venue where players buy and sell shares of other players and NPCs; valuations are driven by reputation and match wins.
EXECUTIVE_PAY
An achievement and profile badge triggered by signing an employment contract worth 500 $VBV or more.
Fence Fee
A 10% tax automatically deducted from the total capital stolen during a successful treasury heist, routed to the Global Faucet.
Forensic Grading
A system (S, A-B, C-F) that tracks the genetic stability of a card based on its history of successful or failed mutations.
Ghost Protocol
A tactical item allowing Outlaws to scramble their signal for 30 minutes, making them undetectable on the Bounty Board.
Global Faucet
The central liquidity pool and redistribution hub for the Arena's economy, funded by various fees and taxes.
Governor Surcharge
A fee equal to 1% of a club's total treasury, paid to the Arena Center Governor when a Regional Governor adjusts local tax policies.
Laundering Tax
A 20% tax applied to ransoms paid during a Kidnap Gambit, routed directly to the Global Faucet.
Lease Board
A platform for earning passive income by renting out idle cards; owners receive 50% of the rental fee.
Mojo
An organization’s social rank, earned through store revenue and heist defenses, which provides buffs to mutation success and employee progression.
Mood Catalyst
A consumable item required for the Mood Recalibration protocol; typically supplied by the Elemental Forge.
Mutation Insurance
A "golden shield" item that guarantees 100% success for gene-editing procedures, preventing Mutation Scars.
Nautilus DEX Path
A backend protocol that converts console revenue into $VBV to pay creators and stakeholders via market-buys.
Reputation
A player’s global standing, which dictates the market price of their shares and determines their matchmaking tier and deck slot limits. All tournament pots, player portfolios, and console voucher values are now strictly `uint64` micro-units.
Role-Gating
A system where access to premium gear or Master Tier status is restricted to players holding specific jobs (Manager, Security, Clerk).
Rumor Mill
A mechanic used to manipulate market sentiments by spending 500 $VBV to increase or decrease a target's reputation and share price.
Sabotage Surcharge
A 500 $VBV penalty paid by a perpetrator directly to a Regional Governor's treasury as reparations for infiltrating their district.
Soul-Bonded
The status achieved when a card reaches 100% loyalty, typically via the Loyalty Synthesis protocol.
Token-Sink Router
The core logic responsible for the atomic redistribution of fees and taxes within the Arena's industrial loop.
Wanted Level
A metric of criminal infamy that rises through heists or tournament misconduct; high levels impose combat power penalties.
