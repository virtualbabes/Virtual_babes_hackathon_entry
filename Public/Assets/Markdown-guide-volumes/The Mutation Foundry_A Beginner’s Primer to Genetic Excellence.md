4. The Price of Failure: Mutation Scars and Power Loss
A failed procedure inflicts a Mutation Scar, a permanent "Genetic Debt" recorded immutably on the blockchain.
Critical Consequences of Failure:
Permanent Power Loss: An immediate, irreversible -50 point reduction to Artifact power. This degradation persists across the entire ecosystem.
Blockchain Archival: Every scar is visible to all players. It cannot be removed, hidden, or repaired.
Valuation Decay: Scars serve as a Forensic Red Flag. They lower the asset's Quality Rating, which negatively impacts the share price on the Nautilus DEX and the Entity Market AMM bonding curve.

5. The Forensic Grading System: Your Asset's Permanent Record
Every asset's history is audited via the Mutation Quality Rating, which dictates its market standing and industrial reliability.
S (Supreme): 100% success rate, zero scars, and full power integrity. This rating is essential for maintaining high asset value in the Entity Market.
A - B (Stable): High reliability with minimal procedural history or minor imperfections.
C - F (Volatile): Multiple permanent scars and significant power degradation. These assets are flagged by the market, resulting in lower liquidity and price.
Audit Protocol: Managers can review the complete forensic ledger of any asset—including every successful realignment and failed scar—within the Gene-Editing Log located in the Player Profile.
