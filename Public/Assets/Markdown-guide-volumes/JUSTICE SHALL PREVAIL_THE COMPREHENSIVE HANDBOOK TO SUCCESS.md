2. The Sovereign Path: Regional Governance and Dominance
The shift from a standard Club Owner to a Regional Governor marks the transition from active management to territorial hegemony. Our governance protocols allow architects of power to collect passive dividends and command the battlefield with superior buffs. Moving into the Master Tier of governance is the only way to achieve true dominance and leverage the labor of subordinates across entire districts.
The Step-to-Governor Protocol
Found a Club: Establish your presence by paying the fixed fee to claim open territory.
Form a Region: Expand your influence to control two or more territories. This is achieved either through direct territorial acquisition or by proposing a Regional Alliance with other owners to share influence.
Achieve Governor Status: Upon managing a region, the owner is granted the title of Regional Governor, unlocking permanent Master Tier status and administrative authority.
Strategic Governance Privileges
+5% Power Buff: Governors grant a direct combat increase to all organization members competing within their home districts.
Alliance Dividends: Governors within a coalition earn a 5% Commission Split from all gene-editing procedures (Vector Realignment, Mood Recalibration, etc.) performed by allied organizations.
20% Regional Governor Tax: Governors claim a 20% cut of all fees paid to the Rumor Mill for market manipulation occurring within their controlled territory.
Infiltration Reparations: Any player successfully sabotaging a Regional Governor is hit with a mandatory 500 $VBV Surcharge, paid directly into the victim’s treasury.
The Arena Center Hegemony
The Governor of the Arena Center occupies the apex of the socio-political hierarchy. This position claims a unique 5% Tournament Tax on the gross prize pools of all automated bracket events. Furthermore, we have instituted a 1% Governor Surcharge levied against any other Regional Governor who enacts or adjusts their localized tax policies. This surcharge is calculated as 1% of the club's total treasury, ensuring that policy shifts carry significant financial consequences for regional leaders.
Political authority is the frame, but it is the technical precision of our economic engine that fuels the expansion of these regional empires.

10% Club Members: Distributed among the lender's club colleagues.
Note: Any mathematical remainders (dust) from these splits are automatically rerouted to the Club Treasury.

The Arena’s "Justice" layer is a socio-economic balancing act. Criminality provides high-risk market access for those with the required Cunning, while law enforcement provides a direct path for "Clean Hunters" to earn $VBV rewards through combat.

The Bounty Board identifies Outlaws (Wanted Level 10+), flagging them for Clean Hunters (Wanted Level 2-). Outlaws may activate the Ghost Protocol (scrambling their signal for 30 minutes), but hunters can override this stealth using a Cloak Disruptor. Victory against a flagged Outlaw yields direct $VBV rewards for the hunter.
Criminality and the Cunning Attribute
High-level criminality requires the Cunning attribute, which scales heist success and mitigates the active power penalties applied to cards when a player has a high Wanted Level.
Black Market Access: To enter the Black Market—where defaulted cards are sold at a discount—a player must possess a Cunning of 10+ and a Wanted Level of 5+.
The Fence Fee: Successful heists are subject to a 10% Fence Fee, which is routed back to the Global Faucet.
Rehabilitation and Bail
Players seeking to restore their standing must visit the Courthouse.
Infamy Clearance: Costs 100 $VBV per Wanted Level to receive the REHABILITATED status.
Jail Bail: If a card is jailed due to a failed heist, a $200 VBV Bail fee must be paid to the capturing club to free it instantly and stop the continuous Reputation drain.
Beyond social standing, the true wealth of a Governor lies in the physical and genetic integrity of their assets.

The Mutation Foundry is where strategic potential is forged into physical reality. Genetic modification is permanent and archived on the blockchain; every procedure defines the asset's forensic value.

All protocols face a 70% Base Success Rate. This is modified by:

Staff Training: Buffs provided by hired Managers, Security, and Clerks.
Sabotage: Procedures performed in a sabotaged club suffer a 15% penalty.
Mutation Insurance: The only method to bypass these risks and guarantee a 100% success rate.
Forensic Grading System
Every card is graded by its genetic history, visible to all players in the Gene-Editing Log.
S (Supreme)
The highest grade, signifying 100% historical success and zero Mutation Scars. This rating reflects a perfect history but does not guarantee future success without the use of Insurance or stability buffs.
A-B (Stable)
Assets with high procedure reliability and minimal historical issues. These assets lack a perfect genetic record but maintain high combat efficiency.
C-F (Volatile)
Assigned to cards with multiple permanent Mutation Scars. Each scar results in a permanent -50 point reduction to Artifact power, representing significant genetic degradation.
