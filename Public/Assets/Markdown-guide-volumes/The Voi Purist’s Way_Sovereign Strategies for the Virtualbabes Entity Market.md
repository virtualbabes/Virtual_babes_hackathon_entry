2. Operational Hierarchy: Scaling the Voi Industrial Loop
The "Industrial Loop" represents the engine of our economy, where participants transition from unaligned Freelancers into specialized roles. These roles are the primary mechanism for scaling club infrastructure and securing territorial dominance.
The Three Primary Operational Roles
Club owners delegate authoritative powers to hired staff to manage commercial and defensive strategy:
Manager (Commercial Strategy): The administrative lead. Managers adjust shop commission rates and monitor inventory logs. Crucially, the presence of a Manager provides the Staff Training buff, a critical stability modifier for genetic mutation procedures.
Security (Defensive Operations): The protective layer. Security staff deploy active sector traps (Laser Tripwires, Sentry Turrets) and manage Bio-Guard Dogs, which jail the rarest cards of failed heisters. Their presence directly scales the difficulty of enemy heists.
Clerk (Revenue Optimization): The efficiency engine. Clerks accelerate store turnover speeds, maximizing the throughput of localized storefronts to drive Mojo accumulation.
The Executive Path to Master Tier Status
Elite status is achieved through territorial control. To reach Master Tier Status, an operator must become a Regional Governor:
Establish a Club: Pay the founding fee to claim territory.
Form a Region: Control two or more territories (individually or via a Regional Alliance).
Governor Appointment: Governors earn Alliance Dividends (a 5% commission split) from all gene-editing performed by allied organizations.
Tactical Combat Buffs:
Regional Boost: Governors grant a +5% power buff to all members in their home districts.
Coalition Defense: Allied members defending a partner’s territory receive a prioritized +10% power boost, which overrides the standard Regional Boost.
The Arena Center Governor: This is the ultimate economic role. The Governor of the Arena Center claims a 1% Governor Surcharge whenever other governors adjust localized tax policies, plus 5% of the gross prize pools from all automated tournaments.

Strategic Influence: The Rumor Mill

Criminality is a calculated path for those who value Cunning over Reputation. High Wanted Levels grant access to the Black Market but invite severe combat power penalties.

Criminals target Club Treasuries in a contest of Cunning vs. Security.

The Bounty Hunter’s Guide
Clean Hunters (Wanted Level ≤ 2) use the Bounty Board to collect $VBV from Outlaws (Wanted Level ≥ 10).
Ghost Protocol: Outlaws pay $VBV to the Tactical Syndicate to become undetectable for 30 minutes.
Cloak Disruptor: Hunters use this item (from Intelligence storefronts) to reveal Outlaws hiding behind Ghost Protocol.
Luxury Tax: All Master Tier/Governor gear purchases (like the Cloak Disruptor) incur a 1% Luxury Tax routed to the Global Faucet.
The Path to Rehabilitation
Criminals can clear their infamy by visiting the Courthouse. By paying 100 $VBV per Wanted Level, a player clears all combat penalties and earns the REHABILITATED status, restoring their standing within the sovereign economy.
