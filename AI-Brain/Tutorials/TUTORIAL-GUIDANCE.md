## Getting Started Tutorial

### Lesson 1: Your First Connection

1. **Open** the game in your browser
2. **Connect Wallet** — Click the wallet button in the top-right
3. **Select Network** — Choose Voi or Algorand
4. **Authorize** — Approve the connection request
5. **Confirm** — Your wallet address appears in the HUD

### Lesson 2: Your First Battle

1. **Open Lobby** — Click "Players & Matchmaking"
2. **Join Queue** — Click "Join Queue"
3. **Wait** — Matchmaking finds an opponent
4. **Battle** — Use attack, defend, and special moves
5. **Win** — Earn $VBV and rating points

### Lesson 3: Your First Purchase

1. **Open Shop** — Click "District Market"
2. **Browse** — View available items
3. **Select** — Choose an item to buy
4. **Confirm** — Approve the transaction
5. **Equip** — Your item is now in your inventory

### Lesson 4: Creating a Club

1. **Open Club Foundry** — Click "Territories" → "Club Foundry"
2. **Name Your Club** — Choose a unique name
3. **Pay Fee** — 5,000 VBV to create
4. **Confirm** — Your club is now active
5. **View** — Your club appears in the Territories panel

### Lesson 5: Purchasing a Territory

1. **Open Territory Map** — Click "Territories" → "Territory Map"
2. **Select District** — Choose an unclaimed district
3. **Pay Fee** — 2,500 VBV (on-chain verified)
4. **Confirm** — Territory is now yours
5. **Repeat** — Buy 2+ territories to become Governor

### Lesson 6: Becoming a Governor

1. **Own 2+ Territories** — Purchase additional territories
2. **Open Regional Manager** — Click "Territories" → "Regional Manager"
3. **Claim Governor** — Click "Claim Governor"
4. **Set Tax** — Choose district tax rate (0-20%)
5. **Manage** — View regional stats and control district shops

### Lesson 7: Opening a Church

1. **Open Faith Church** — Click "Faith" → "Faith Church"
2. **Name Church** — Choose a unique name
3. **Choose Dogma** — Purist, Syncretic, or Orthodox
4. **Pay Fee** — Opening cost (VBV)
5. **Confirm** — Your church is now active

### Lesson 8: Performing Rituals

1. **Open Your Church** — Click "Faith" → "Faith Church" → "My Churches"
2. **Select Ritual** — Choose a ritual type
3. **Pay Fee** — 0.1 $VBV per ritual
4. **Confirm** — Ritual performed, FaithCoherence rises
5. **View** — Check regional FaithCoherence impact

### Lesson 9: Creating AI Citizens

1. **Open AI Citizens** — Click "AI Citizens"
2. **Spawn AI** — Click "Spawn AI Citizen"
3. **Configure** — Set behavioral parameters
4. **Confirm** — AI spawns with own wallet
5. **Manage** — Monitor and direct AI activities

### Lesson 10: Investing in Entities

1. **Open Entity Shares** — Click "Entity Investments" → "Shares"
2. **Browse Tokens** — View available share tokens
3. **Buy Shares** — Purchase shares in entities
4. **Track** — Monitor your holdings
5. **Earn** — Receive dividends from entity success

### Lesson 11: Creating DLC

1. **Open Creator Store** — Click "Creator Economy"
2. **Create DLC** — Click "Create DLC"
3. **Configure** — Set title, description, price
4. **Publish** — DLC is now available for sale
5. **Earn** — Receive 90% royalty on each sale

### Lesson 12: Backing a Launch

1. **Open Launchpad** — Click "Creator Economy" → "Launchpad"
2. **Browse Launches** — View active launches
3. **Back Launch** — Contribute VBV to a project
4. **Track** — Monitor launch progress
5. **Profit** — If funded, receive rewards

## Advanced Tutorials

### Advanced 1: Faith War Gambit

1. **Stake Card** — Choose your favorite card
2. **Challenge** — Challenge another faith
3. **Battle** — Stats + rivalry bonuses determine outcome
4. **Win** — Card returns + winnings
5. **Lose** — Card jailed to winning faith

### Advanced 2: Regional Governance

1. **Define Rivalry Sets** — Set regional relationships
2. **Host Events** — Create regional tournaments
3. **Allocate Treasury** — Fund regional development
4. **Tax Strategy** — Optimize tax revenue
5. **Diplomacy** — Negotiate with other governors

### Advanced 3: Stat Optimization

1. **View Stats** — Open Stat Overlay panel
2. **Identify Weakness** — Find lowest stats
3. **Train** — Use events/rituals to boost stats
4. **Stack** — Combine multiple stat boosts
5. **Compete** — Enter high-tier tournaments

### Advanced 4: Entity Market Trading

1. **List Entity** — Put pet/children bot/vehicle for sale
2. **Set Price** — Choose VBV amount
3. **Wait** — Buyers browse your listing
4. **Sell** — Accept offer, transfer ownership
5. **Profit** — Earn VBV from successful trades

### Advanced 5: Infrastructure Leasing

1. **Browse Systems** — View available OS modules
2. **Lease Module** — Pay monthly rate
3. **Integrate** — Use leased system
4. **Renew** — Auto-renew or cancel
5. **Scale** — Lease more modules as needed

### Advanced 6: Compliance Management

1. **View Records** — Check compliance status
2. **Create Record** — Flag suspicious activity
3. **Resolve** — Address compliance issues
4. **Escalate** — Send to admin if critical
5. **Audit** — Review historical records
