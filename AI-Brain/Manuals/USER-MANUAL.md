## Getting Started

### What is NFT-Seduction?

NFT-Seduction is a persistent, self-sustaining, cross-platform digital civilization where games, creators, businesses, investors, developers, AI entities, and communities coexist inside a shared economic infrastructure.

### Core Principles

- **Player Freedom:** You control your assets, your path, your destiny
- **Economic Depth:** Every action feeds into a living economy
- **Social Interaction:** Build relationships, join clubs, create communities
- **Long-term Sustainability:** Value circulates forever — nothing disappears
- **Creator Opportunity:** Create, sell, lease, and earn
- **Persistent History:** Your actions build permanent reputation

### First Steps

1. **Connect Wallet** — Link your Algorand or Voi wallet
2. **Create Identity** — Set up your persistent player profile
3. **Join the Arena** — Enter matchmaking, battle other players
4. **Build Reputation** — Your actions create permanent history
5. **Join a Club** — Find or create a club, purchase territories
6. **Explore** — Discover events, markets, AI citizens, and more

## Core Gameplay

### Matchmaking

- Join the queue via the **Lobby** panel
- Battle other players in real-time combat
- Win to earn $VBV rewards and rating points
- Lose to learn and improve

### Combat System

- **Challenge** other players directly
- **Move** during matches (attack, defend, special)
- **Rating** tracks your competitive success
- **XP** unlocks new tiers and abilities

### Tournaments

- **Register** for autonomous tournaments (every 15 minutes)
- **Bracket** system with elimination rounds
- **Rewards** for winners (VBV + stat bonuses)
- **History** preserved permanently

## Economy & Currency

### $VBV — The Primary Currency

- **$VBV** is the main in-game currency
- All purchases, trades, and rewards use $VBV
- Earned through: battles, tournaments, events, trading, creating
- Stored in your wallet (Algorand/Voi blockchain)

### Other Tokens

- **Nugget** — Used in Nugget shops only
- **Unit** — Used in Unit shops only
- Both are paid via Algorand mainnet, triggering backend $VBV

### The Industrial Loop

Value circulates forever:
```
Player Activity → Businesses → Employment → Purchasing → Taxes → Treasuries → Development → Events → Player Activity
```

## Clubs & Territories

### Creating a Club

1. Open **Club Foundry** (5,000 VBV)
2. Name your club
3. Your club starts with 1 territory

### Purchasing Territories

- Each additional territory costs 2,500 VBV (on-chain verified)
- Own 2+ territories to become a **Governor**
- Governors can set district taxes (0-20%)

### Regional Manager

- Governors can open the **Regional Manager** panel
- Manage territories, set taxes, view regional stats
- Regional roles refresh at season rollover

### Club Types

Clubs can evolve into:
- **Company** — Run businesses, hire employees
- **Government** — Regional governance, taxation
- **Crime Syndicate** — Underworld contracts, heists
- **Sports Team** — Tournament focus
- **Investment Firm** — Invest in entities
- **Educational Institution** — Train AI citizens
- **Nation** — Multi-territory empire
- **Community** — Social focus
- **Media Brand** — Content creation

## Faith & Religion

### Opening a Church

1. Open **Faith Church** panel
2. Name your church
3. Choose dogma: **Purist**, **Syncretic**, or **Orthodox**
4. Pay opening cost (VBV)
5. You are now the church leader

### Religion Governance

- **24-cap** — Server-wide maximum of 24 religions
- **Faucet-owned** — First 24 religions sold cheap by faucet
- **Buyout** — Buy another religion at 10% above last price
- **Total Conquest** — Old owner loses everything, all members auto-convert
- **Faucet-directed** — All buyout money → faucet (not old owner)
- **Converted users** — Can ONLY buy out another religion to return
- **Restore or Create** — On buyout, choose: restore old OR create new

### Rituals

- Perform rituals to raise **FaithCoherence**
- Cost: 0.1 $VBV per ritual → faucet
- Higher FaithCoherence = more regional influence

### Faith War Gambit

- Stake your favorite card in religious battles
- Win → card returns + winnings
- Lose → card jailed to winning faith's kitty

## Rivalry System

### Rivalry Factions

- **Vehicle Builders** vs **Breeders** — Main rivalry
- Bonuses apply in pet battles
- Career rivalries stack across vehicle builders

### Rivalry Effects

- **XP Modifiers** — Bonus XP for rival-faith actions
- **Battle Bonuses** — +40 for rival-faith, -30 for same-faith
- **Regional Influence** — Rivalries affect regional dynamics

### Defining Rivalry Sets

- Governors can define rivalry sets between religions
- Creates strategic depth in regional politics

## Market & Trading

### Entity Market

- **Public listings** for pets, children bots, vehicles
- **Trade history** + ownership transfer
- **Showcase-only** (price=0) or for sale

- **Entity Shares** — Invest in players, businesses, creators, guilds, projects, communities

- **Dividends** — Earn from share holdings
