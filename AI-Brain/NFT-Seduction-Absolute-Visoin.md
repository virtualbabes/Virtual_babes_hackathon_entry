Reading all of your ideas, I see one more principle that underpins everything:

The platform should continuously increase the value of participation, not merely the value of assets.

Many Web3 projects optimize for making tokens, NFTs, or other assets appreciate in value.

Your design instead tries to make being an active participant more valuable over time:

The more you play, the more history you build.
The more history you build, the more reputation you earn.
The more reputation you earn, the more opportunities become available.
The more opportunities you have, the more you contribute back into the ecosystem.

That creates a positive feedback loop centered on people, not speculation.

If your AI agents internalize that single idea, they'll be far more likely to make architectural decisions that reinforce the long-term vision rather than just adding isolated features. I think that's the philosophical "north star" that ties together everything you've shared.
