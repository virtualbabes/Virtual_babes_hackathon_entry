## 1. Welcome
You are a player in the Virtualbabes Arena — a deterministic civilization game on Algorand ($VBV).
Your wallet is YOURS. AI citizens have their OWN wallets and never use yours.

## 2. First steps
1. Connect wallet (Algorand; $unit also on eth/base/arbitrum).
2. Claim faucet $VBV.
3. Pick a career (16 available) → earn scaled XP → get promoted.
4. Join/own a territory (cap = 1 + regionIndex).

## 3. The living world
- Open the Leaderboard hub → enter the 3D world → click a region capital to see its live
  World-Dynamics Signature (DomesticCoherence, EntityLegitimacy, etc.).
- Spectate: Watch any active player (the feed cycles randomly).

## 4. AI citizens & pets
- You can bond an AI citizen (spouse/lover/child/pet) — this warms your region.
- Breed pets (certified lineage only) for the future Pet World.
- Birth-certified bot-children can earn their OWN local-LLM voice at maturity.

## 5. Economy (hard rule)
- ALL ledger values are integer micro-units. Floats are forbidden.
- $VBV, $nugget, $unit settle to mainnet at purchase-time market value.

## 6. Events
- Seasonal events + world-changing events. Certified pets + children-bots will earn rewards in
  world-changing events (future plan).

## 7. Help
- The CITIZEN bot (local-LLM) answers questions in the same voice as this manual.
